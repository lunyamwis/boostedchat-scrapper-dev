Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

Introduction

Lunyamwi Gmail Chatbot (“the App”) helps small businesses automate email replies, manage Gmail inboxes, and run simple marketing campaigns. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Information We Access

Our app uses Gmail API scopes to read, compose, and send emails on your behalf. We may access the following types of data, with your explicit consent through Google OAuth:

  • Email Metadata: Message headers, subject lines, and labels to detect and categorize new messages.
  • Email Content (Read-Only): To understand message context and generate automated responses.
  • Compose and Send: To send replies and marketing messages when you instruct the chatbot or run a campaign.

How We Use the Information

The information accessed from Gmail is used solely to:

  • Display incoming messages and generate automated responses.
  • Compose and send emails or campaigns when authorized by you.
  • Provide analytics or summaries of communication activity (e.g., campaign reports).

Data Storage and Security

We do not permanently store your email content or metadata on our servers. Any temporary data is securely deleted after processing. Access tokens are encrypted and stored securely to maintain your session with Gmail.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your data with any third party except where required by law or explicitly authorized by you.

User Control

You can revoke the app’s access to your Gmail account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page.

Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
